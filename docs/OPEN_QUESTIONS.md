# Open Questions

Open questions, TBDs, and parked decisions are tracked privately with the
operator notes, not in this public repo. Answers that shape the product are
recorded as ADRs in [adr/](adr/README.md). The work queue is [TODO.md](TODO.md).

**Promotion rule:** Do not open a question for an optional tool with a safe
default (skip install, or pin only). Use catalog I1 and a priority instead.
Promote architectural answers with `/adr`.

New projects created with `bootstrap/project-process` get their own
`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` and private ops notes from its templates.
