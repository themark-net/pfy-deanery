## Stage card gap

The catalog loop's queue (`scripts/catalog_eval_queue.py`) reads only `data/stages/*.json`.
An entry without a card never reaches the queue. The newest card before this batch pointed at
entry 181, so entries 182-199 that have no card are also outside the queue. That backfill is
left for a separate change, because each card needs a checked `uses`, `next_gate` and pin.

New cards carry no `tier_ceiling` and no `promotion_history`; the loop's rules add those.
`data/stages/_registry.json` coverage, the dashboard and `eval/catalog-queue/queue.tsv` are loop
outputs and are regenerated by the catalog-counts run, not by hand.

Queue mode from `next_gate`: LoopForge, CompozyOS and vibecosystem are `ref` (design reference,
no install). Thermite, Agent of Empires and agent-custody are `trial`.
